The engine

What BruteX does

Instead of guessing a strategy and testing it, BruteX defines the full language of market conditions — then tests everything.

60 conditions

Market vocabulary

A structured vocabulary of 60 atomic market conditions — pivots, Fibonacci levels, VWAP position, market structure, day types — combined mechanically, not by intuition.

~33 billion trials

Every combination searched

The full combinatorial space is enumerated — smart pruning kills ~95% of dead branches so the search stays exhaustive without being wasteful.

The rigor moat

What even most pros skip

Searching billions of combinations guarantees you will find things that look great by pure luck. The moat is the machinery that kills those illusions.

Deflated Sharpe + CPCV

Overfitting guards built into ranking — a strategy must beat LUCK, not just look green on a chart.

Bit-for-bit reproducible

Every run is hash-keyed on its exact inputs. Re-run it next year and get identical results, byte for byte.

Hash-chained audit trail

Every material action appends to a tamper-evident, hash-chained log. Breaking the chain fails the build.

Correct-or-absent data

If a bar cannot be verified, it is honestly absent — never interpolated, never fabricated, never silently wrong.

Look-ahead-proof

The engine can only see the past at every bar — enforced by runtime guards and property tests, not by promise.

Honest cost labeling

Signal-only results are clearly marked signal-only. No quietly ignoring costs and calling it edge.

Honest limits

What BruteX does not claim

  • Signal-only P&L today. Current results measure raw signal quality; the options-translation track models real trading costs.
  • Bounded vocabulary. 60 conditions is deep, not infinite — patterns outside the vocabulary are outside the search.
  • Data from 2020. Roughly 6.5 years of minute history — long enough for statistics, short of a full multi-decade cycle.
  • Past patterns ≠ future returns. Every surviving strategy still requires forward validation before any live use.
