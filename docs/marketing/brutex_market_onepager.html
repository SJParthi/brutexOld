The engine

What BruteX does

Most traders test a handful of hunches. BruteX defines the full language of market conditions — then tests every combination.

60 conditions

Market vocabulary

Sixty simple building blocks — like “price is above yesterday’s high” — that combine into every strategy the engine tests.

A structured vocabulary of 60 atomic market conditions — pivots, Fibonacci levels, VWAP position, market structure, day types — combined mechanically, not by intuition.

730B → 33B

Searches everything — in stages

Instead of trusting a handful of hunches, BruteX tries every possible combination of its rules — billions of them.

A staged funnel takes the raw 730-billion candidate space down to ~33 billion (3,300 crore) hot-path backtests: contradictory condition pairs are pruned upfront (~50% of masks — a pattern that can never fire is never run) and every trial is O(1)-deduplicated. Every surviving candidate is backtested to completion — never early-stopped.

The rigor moat

What even most pros skip

Searching billions of combinations guarantees you will find things that look great by pure luck. The moat is the machinery that kills those illusions.

Catches lucky flukes

A rule that only looked good by luck gets caught and thrown out.

Overfitting guards built into the pipeline — Deflated Sharpe (DSR) gates the leaderboard, and a nine-filter statistical cascade (CPCV, SPA, PBO, walk-forward, lockbox, Monte-Carlo among them) runs automatically on every sweep’s winners.

Same answer, every time

Run it again next year — you get the exact same answer, provably.

Every run is hash-keyed (blake3) on its exact inputs — data, code and parameters. Re-run it and get identical results, byte for byte.

Everything on the record

Every action the system takes is written down in a ledger no one can quietly edit.

Every material action appends one line to a tamper-evident, hash-chained JSONL audit log. Breaking the chain fails the build.

Bad data gets rejected

If the data looks wrong, it’s thrown out loudly — never quietly patched.

Correct-or-absent, fail-loud: a corrupt bar fails the run rather than slipping through; suspect values are shown absent rather than wrong. Nothing is interpolated, nothing is fabricated.

Never peeks at the future

The engine can never peek at tomorrow’s prices — tested, not promised.

Look-ahead-proof: at every bar the engine can only see the past — enforced by runtime guards and property tests, not by promise.

Honest cost labeling

If a result ignores trading costs, it says so — right on the label.

Signal-only results are clearly marked signal-only. No quietly ignoring costs and calling it edge.

Honest limits

What BruteX does not claim

Every tool has edges. These are BruteX’s — stated plainly, not buried in fine print.

  • Signal-only P&L today. Current results measure raw signal quality; the options-translation track models real trading costs.
  • Bounded vocabulary. 60 conditions is deep, not infinite — patterns outside the vocabulary are outside the search.
  • Data from 2020. Roughly 6.5 years of minute history — long enough for statistics, short of a full multi-decade cycle.
  • Past patterns ≠ future returns. Every surviving strategy still requires forward validation before any live use.
