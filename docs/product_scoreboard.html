BruteX

A research engine that records every single minute of India's index & F&O market history (2020 → today), shows you what's actually moving right now, and brute-force tests trading ideas at massive scale — built so nothing is ever slow, nothing is ever lost, and you always know what's happening.

~750tradable names in the data lake
21timeframes, all from 1-minute data
2020→todayminute-by-minute history
100%line + branch test coverage

Scope note (honest): the brute-force engine trades the 3 indices (NIFTY / BANKNIFTY / SENSEX); the ~750-name lake is reference data for future expansion. Nothing in the engine scope is ever changed silently.
