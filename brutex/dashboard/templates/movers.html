ⓘ What am I looking at?
Each row is one index/stock at the selected date, timeframe and as-of time. LTP is the last traded price at (or before) the as-of instant; Change / Change % compare it to the previous NSE session's close; Volume and Value (₹ crore) sum the session's trading up to the as-of cutoff. A trailing " *" on a change cell means the baseline is not the immediately-preceding session (a gap in the stored data — the nearest prior session was used).
Each row is one futures contract of the selected expiry tier (Near = the closest unexpired month, Next = the second, Far = the third). Premium is the contract price minus the underlying spot. OI (open interest) is read at the as-of instant and OI Chg % compares it to the prior day's OI. The Buildup pill combines price and OI direction: price up + OI up reads Long Buildup, price down + OI up Short Buildup, price up + OI down Short Covering, price down + OI down Long Unwinding — "—" when either input is missing or flat. Volume/Value sum the session up to the as-of cutoff; " *" marks a change computed against a non-adjacent prior session.
Each row is one option contract at the tier expiry on the chosen cadence track (All/Monthly/Weekly). OI (open interest) is read at the as-of instant; Volume sums the session up to the as-of cutoff; Change % is vs the contract's previous close and the OI-change columns are vs its prior day. The Put/Call Ratio chips divide the summed put-side OI (or volume) by the call side across every chain shown — a ratio of sums; click a ratio chip for the per-underlying detail. Coverage counts contracts actually read out of the contracts discovered for the tier; "missing OI" counts read contracts whose stored OI cell is empty (click the amber badge to see which). The all-exchanges view folds both listings of an underlying into one row (the population the table and the chips rank) — pick an exchange for a per-listing view. The Buildup pill reads price vs OI direction as on Futures. " *" marks a change computed against a non-adjacent prior session; " ~" marks a strike grid estimated from stored data only.
