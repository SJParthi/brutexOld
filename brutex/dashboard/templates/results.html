Per-period max-DD is segmented within the period — a year's DD is NOT the max of its months' and understates boundary-spanning drawdowns; the row's max-drawdown column remains the authoritative risk figure. Half-year DD is not stored — shown as —. Yr-gate chips are DISPLAY verdicts at current default thresholds derived from stored buckets; the rank-time gate config of each run is recorded in the sweep audit chain. Year-gating ADDS a calendar-aligned constraint on top of DSR/CPCV/WFO — it does NOT remove curve-fitting (at defaults it passes ~50% of pure noise over 7 years). — = no stored data / not judged. DSR (tuple-N) is the persisted per-tuple Deflated Sharpe — deflated against ONE grid cell's rank-time candidate count, an inflating under-count, so it is never shown unlabeled; DSR @ search N is the Stage-4 cascade's own filter-#2 figure at its recorded trial population (hover the cell for N + source; a "pool"-sourced figure shares the under-count and pairs with the ~ provisional badge).
