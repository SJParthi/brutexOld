Spot pulls 1-minute from 2020-01-01 → previous trading day — 1-minute is the only granularity fetched from Groww; 1-day is DERIVED from it (the engine's daily anchor), never separately fetched. Options pull ALL vendor-listed strikes per expiry — the vendor chain (get_expiries → get_contracts) returns every listed strike, not relative-to-spot · CE+PE · weekly+monthly · near/next/far, 1-minute (1-day derived). Re-click any time — already-pulled windows are skipped (no wasted Groww hit). The 19 intraday timeframes (and 1-day) are derived from the 1-minute base and written only by the pipeline's automatic FINAL pre-build stage once the fetch fully succeeds — covering SPOT, FUTURES and OPTIONS (F&O keeps open-interest). Heads-up on storage: a fully pre-built lake costs a measured multiple of its 1-minute footprint — the pipeline measures the real multiplier after every pre-build and uses a conservative 6.5× estimate before the first measurement (the earlier "2–3×" figure ignored the Greeks-enriched option 1-minute rewrite; DEC-LAKE-FOOTPRINT-MEASURED-001, operator-accepted; the lake is your external drive via BRUTEX_LAKE_ROOT). A pre-run disk guard skips the pre-build when free space is short (BRUTEX_PREBUILD_DISK_GUARD=off to override). There is no manual pre-build button — the pipeline runs it itself.
