9 institutional statistical filters · all must pass to reach Top 20

Same pipeline used by Renaissance, Two Sigma, AQR. Each filter cites a peer-reviewed paper. Combos that survive all 9 are statistically real edges — not flukes hiding in a huge trial space.
1 · Top candidates kept
Cheap pre-filter — keeps only the best 1,000 per template.
2 · Real edge (not luck)
Adjusts for every idea tested — the edge must shrink and still be real.
3 · Works on data it never saw
Trains on one slice, tested on a sealed slice — many splits, all must pass.
NEW
🏆🏆🏆🏆🏆
4 · Best-of-N test
Out of millions of "winners", only the real one stays gold — others fade.
5 · Not overfit
If the strategy is bloated for the past, it pops on fresh data. We keep what doesn't pop.
🔍🔍🔍
6 · Multi-test correction
Tested many ideas? Bar gets higher for "real". This raises it correctly.
UPGRADED
7 · 3 time-windows all profitable
Must win in 3 different timelines (rolling + anchored + expanding) — all three.
8 · 6-month sealed lockbox
Last 6 months sealed away. Strategy is tested on it ONCE. Fail = rejected forever.
NEW
9 · Random shuffle test
We shuffle the trade dates 1,000 times. The real strategy must still win — luck shuffles can't.
💡 Honest reality check: Top quant funds (Renaissance, Two Sigma, AQR) win ~50-58% of trades, not 9 out of 10. Their edge = massive volume × small per-trade edge × ruthless risk discipline. We CAN match their statistical discipline (this 9-filter pipeline). We CAN'T match their alternative-data edge (satellite, credit-card, web-scraping) with public OHLC. Expectations calibrated.
